About Us

Who We Are

We are a grassroots, parent-led volunteer organization based right here in Austin. We are not professional or certified advocates, and we do not charge any fees. Instead, we are special needs parents who have spent years in the trenches navigating ARD meetings, 504 plans, and the public school system.

Small by design.

Because we work on a completely volunteer basis, we only accept a select number of families each month. This ensures we can give every parent and child the dedicated time, energy, and personal attention they deserve.

Who We Serve

Who We Are a Fit For

Navigating school accommodations can be overwhelming, especially when hiring a private advocate is out of your budget. We are here to bridge that gap. Our volunteer services are exclusively for:

Austin Residents

Families with children currently enrolled in or entering Austin-area public schools and surrounding cities.

Financial Need

Parents who genuinely cannot afford to hire a professional, paid advocate.

Specific Classifications

Families navigating school accommodations for children with:

Other Health Impairments (OHI) Emotional Disturbance (ED) ADHD Anxiety
What We Do

How We Help

We don't offer legal advice, but we do offer real-world experience, empathy, and a guiding hand. We partner with you to make sure your child's voice is heard.

Document Review

Helping you make sense of IEP paperwork, 504 plans, and evaluation reports.

Meeting Preparation

Organizing your talking points, goals, and questions before you walk into the school.

ARD & 504 Support

Attending school meetings alongside you as a knowledgeable peer support system.

Resource Sharing

Connecting you with local Austin special needs networks and community resources.

We are volunteer parents, not attorneys or certified advocates, and we do not provide legal advice.
